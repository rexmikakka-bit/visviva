Privacy Policy

Axis — EVE Online fitting calculator · Last updated 16 August 2026

Axis does not collect your personal data. There is no account to create, no server operated by the developer, and no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software of any kind in the app. Everything you create stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

Your ship fits, application settings, character skill levels and (if you choose to sign in with EVE Online) your access tokens are saved in your device's local app storage. They are never uploaded to the developer, because there is nowhere to upload them to. Deleting the app removes them.

Fits you explicitly export — as a file, as a shared image, or to your EVE character's in-game saved fittings — go only where you send them.

Signing in with EVE Online (optional)

Axis is fully usable without signing in. If you choose to connect a character, sign-in is handled by Fenris Creations' own EVE Single Sign-On. Your EVE username and password are entered on Fenris Creations' website and are never seen by, or transmitted through, this app. Axis receives only an access token, which is stored on your device and used to read your character's skills and to read and write your in-game saved fittings, at your request. You can disconnect a character at any time in Settings, which erases the stored token, and you can revoke access permanently at developers.eveonline.com.

Network connections

Axis contacts the following services. As with any internet request, these services necessarily receive your device's IP address in order to reply. Axis sends them no other identifying information.

These are independent services with their own privacy practices, which this policy does not cover. No request to any of them includes your name, email address or any identifier created by Axis.

Children

Axis is a utility for players of EVE Online and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.
