# What's New in Axis 1.26.4

Changes since iOS 1.26.3:

- Added the Crimson Harvest items: the Akoman battlecruiser, Horm's Capital Energy Neutralizer, Sarikusa's Capital Energy Nosferatu, Zorya's Ultratidal Entropic Disintegrator, Abyssal Remote Armor Repairers and their mutaplasmids, and the new event boosters.
- Ships in Siege, Triage or Bastion mode, or running an Industrial Core, now receive almost no incoming remote repairs or remote capacitor, matching the game.
- Abyssal Remote Armor Repairers can be imported, and sorted by repair rate and repair per GJ.
- Fixed the Minokawa's capital remote capacitor bonus, which was not being applied.
- Updated Paladin and Golem agility to CCP's corrected values.
- New ships now appear in ship search.
