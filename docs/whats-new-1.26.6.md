# What's New in Axis 1.26.6

Changes since iOS 1.26.4:

- Fixed T2 ammo staying loaded after swapping guns to a T1 or meta variant. A gun now only keeps ammo it can actually load, and pasted EFT fits drop ammo the gun can't take.
- Fits saved with ammo their guns can't load now show it in Validation, and that ammo no longer counts toward damage.
- The cargo hold now counts toward the fit's value, with its own Cargo row.
- Loaded ammo is now priced for the whole load instead of one round per gun.
