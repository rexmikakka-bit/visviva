# What's New in Axis 1.25.2

Changes since 1.22.6:

- **Abyssal modules, together.** Import your characters' owned abyssals, browse by module group or container, add favorites and custom names, and save custom rolls. Compare your collection with live MutaMarket listings directly in Variations.
- **Better abyssal comparisons.** Sort by useful attributes and derived stats, lock attributes to keep upgrades, filter by fitting space and budget, and independently include auctions or multi-item contracts. Prices, ownership, locations and contract types are clearly labelled.
- **From comparison to shopping list.** Add a market roll to your fit, see its asking price in the fit value, and copy individual or combined EVE contract links without connecting a character. Saved modules and contract details remain available offline.
- **Smarter ammunition choices.** Compare ammo from the Firepower card using damage against your selected target, with DPS, volley, range, overheat and compact missile damage-type comparisons.
- **Smoother fitting.** Redo joins Undo, module duplication supports multiple slots, drones can be added in flights, cargo contents and remaining space are easier to see, and module, cargo and drone browsing have improved navigation.
- **More accurate calculations.** Corrected hull statistics, warp speeds and hardpoints; improved fighter graph coverage; fixed boosted scrambler ranges in damage graphs and stealth-bomber launcher fitting indicators.
- **Eight interface languages.** English, German, French, Spanish, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese. EVE item names stay in English for fitting compatibility.
- **A fresh abyssal app icon**, with mobile layout and usability refinements throughout.
