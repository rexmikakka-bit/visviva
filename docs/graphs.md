# Graphs

The Graphs tab plots one fit's performance against distance, time, target speed or signature, depending on the graph.

1. Pick a category: Damage, Ewar, Reps, Shield, Capacitor, Mobility, Warp or Lock. The chips scroll sideways.
2. Choose the **Y axis** and **X axis** from the dropdowns. Use **−** / **+** to rescale either axis.
3. Tap the chart to move the cursor. The big number is the reading there (`34.2 DPS @ 81.0`).
4. For damage, set the **Target profile**: Ideal, Frigate, Cruiser or Battleship, or **Choose target fit…** to shoot at one of your saved fits. Each preset fills in **Sig radius** and **Speed** (Frigate is 40 m at 350 m/s). **MWD** switches it to the MWD-blooming version (200 m at 3050 m/s). Both fields can be edited.

If the presets barely change the curve, look under **Flight vectors**. By default the target flies straight along your line of sight (0 m/s transversal), so turret tracking doesn't come into it.
